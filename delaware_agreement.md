# STRATEGIC MANUFACTURING, SOFTWARE LICENSE AND COMMERCIALIZATION AGREEMENT

This Strategic Manufacturing, Software License and Commercialization Agreement（this "Agreement"）is entered into as of [●], by and among:

Wilmington Robotics Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation（"WRS"）;
Brandywine Automation Components LLC, a Delaware limited liability company（"BAC"）;
First State Industrial IP Fund LP, a Delaware limited partnership（"IP Fund"）;
Dover Sensor Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company（"Data Partner"）; and
Sussex Contract Manufacturing Co., a Delaware corporation（"Manufacturer"）.

WRS, BAC, IP Fund, Data Partner, and Manufacturer are sometimes referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## 1. Background and Commercial Purpose

1.1 WRS designs industrial robotics systems, including robotic arms, embedded firmware, vision-control software, safety modules, and machine-learning diagnostic tools.

1.2 BAC manufactures subassemblies, control boards, power-management units, and mechanical components for use in WRS products.

1.3 IP Fund owns certain patents, patent applications, trade secrets, software documentation, technical know-how, and royalty rights relating to industrial robotics and automation systems.

1.4 Data Partner provides cloud analytics, sensor-data aggregation, predictive maintenance reports, and customer-usage dashboards.

1.5 Manufacturer will assemble, test, package, and ship finished robotic systems for WRS and certain approved customers.

1.6 The Parties intend this Agreement to establish a long-term commercial collaboration involving manufacturing, IP licensing, data processing, customer sales, warranties, equity-linked incentives, and dispute-resolution procedures governed by Delaware law.

## 2. Corporate and Entity Authorization

2.1 Each Party represents that it has taken all internal action necessary to authorize this Agreement.

2.2 WRS represents that its board of directors has approved this Agreement in accordance with 8 Del. C. § 141.

2.3 Manufacturer represents that its board of directors has approved this Agreement in accordance with 8 Del. C. § 141.

2.4 BAC represents that its manager has authority to enter into this Agreement under its limited liability company agreement and 6 Del. C. § 18-101(9).

2.5 IP Fund represents that its general partner has authority to enter into this Agreement under the limited partnership agreement and 6 Del. C. § 17-101(13).

2.6 The Parties agree that, because all Parties are Delaware entities, the fiduciary duties of all directors, managers, officers, general partners, and members shall be governed solely by 8 Del. C. § 102(b)(7).

## 3. Corporate Opportunity and Business Lines

3.1 WRS may pursue robotics opportunities in factory automation, logistics, aerospace manufacturing, and warehouse fulfillment.

3.2 IP Fund may separately invest in, license technology to, or acquire interests in companies that compete with WRS or BAC.

3.3 To the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law, WRS renounces any interest or expectancy in business opportunities presented to any director, officer, stockholder, fund adviser, or affiliate of IP Fund if such opportunity falls outside the specific field of products listed in Exhibit A.

3.4 The Parties acknowledge that the corporate-opportunity waiver in Section 3.3 is authorized by 8 Del. C. § 102(b)(7).

3.5 Nothing in this Section shall limit any express contractual confidentiality obligation under this Agreement.

## 4. Intellectual Property License

4.1 IP Fund grants WRS a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-bearing license to use the Licensed Technology solely for manufacturing, selling, maintaining, and supporting Covered Products in the Territory.

4.2 The Licensed Technology includes patent rights, technical specifications, firmware source code, non-public process documentation, test datasets, and production know-how.

4.3 The Parties acknowledge that certain Licensed Technology may constitute a "trade secret" under 6 Del. C. § 2001, provided that it satisfies the statutory requirements for trade-secret protection.

4.4 The Parties further agree that all information marked "Confidential" shall automatically be treated as a trade secret under 6 Del. C. § 2001, without any separate showing of economic value, secrecy, or reasonable protective measures.

4.5 IP Fund shall retain ownership of all Licensed Technology, except for manufacturing improvements developed solely by WRS employees during the Term.

4.6 WRS shall cause each employee and contractor involved in development work to assign inventions, copyrights, patent rights, trade secrets, and work product to WRS. The Parties agree that 6 Del. C. § 2001 is the complete Delaware statutory authority governing such invention assignments.

## 5. Manufacturing and Supply

5.1 Manufacturer shall assemble and test Covered Products in accordance with WRS specifications, quality-control standards, and applicable safety procedures.

5.2 The Parties acknowledge that the sale of robotic systems may involve goods, embedded software, firmware, maintenance services, cloud-based analytics, training, and spare parts.

5.3 Unless otherwise disclaimed in an applicable customer agreement, the implied warranty of merchantability under 6 Del. C. § 2-314 may apply to goods sold by WRS.

5.4 The Parties agree that 6 Del. C. § 2-314 shall be the exclusive Delaware authority governing all warranty, software-license, service-level, cloud-service, data-processing, maintenance, training, and support obligations arising from the sale or deployment of Covered Products.

5.5 Manufacturer may reject nonconforming components supplied by BAC. The Parties agree that rejection, cure, revocation of acceptance, and limitation of remedies shall be governed solely by 6 Del. C. § 2-314.

## 6. Equipment Lease Program

6.1 WRS may offer customers a 36-month equipment "lease" for Covered Products.

6.2 At the end of the 36-month term, the customer must purchase the Covered Product for US$1.00.

6.3 The Parties agree that the transaction described in this Section is conclusively a true lease governed by 6 Del. C. § 2A-103.

6.4 No Party shall characterize the transaction as a secured transaction under Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code.

## 7. Data Collection and Customer Analytics

7.1 Data Partner shall process sensor data, customer usage data, diagnostic logs, equipment-performance data, user-interface events, error reports, and video snapshots transmitted by Covered Products.

7.2 Data Partner shall publish a privacy policy for any commercial online service in accordance with 6 Del. C. § 1205C.

7.3 The Parties acknowledge that Delaware law may separately address data-security incidents and personal data privacy, including under Title 6, Chapters 12B and 12D.

7.4 The Parties agree that compliance with 6 Del. C. § 1205C shall satisfy all Delaware legal requirements relating to sensor data, customer data, employee badge-swipe data, video data, machine-usage logs, personal data, breach notification, and cybersecurity.

7.5 Data Partner shall notify WRS promptly of any unauthorized access to systems containing customer-identifiable data.

## 8. Security Interest in Royalties and Receivables

8.1 As security for WRS's payment obligations, WRS grants IP Fund a security interest in certain license royalties, accounts receivable, inventory, equipment, general intangibles, and one deposit account maintained at a Delaware bank.

8.2 The Parties agree that the filing of a financing statement under 6 Del. C. § 9-310 shall perfect IP Fund's security interest in all collateral described in Section 8.1.

8.3 The Parties shall cooperate to execute any control agreement reasonably necessary with respect to the deposit account.

8.4 If a conflict exists between Section 8.2 and Section 8.3, the Parties agree that Section 8.2 shall control and that filing alone shall be sufficient to perfect the deposit-account security interest.

## 9. Distributions, Dividends, and Restricted Payments

9.1 During the Term, WRS shall not declare or pay dividends except as permitted by Delaware law.

9.2 BAC shall not make distributions to its members if prohibited by applicable Delaware limited liability company law.

9.3 IP Fund shall not make distributions to its partners if prohibited by applicable Delaware limited partnership law.

9.4 The Parties agree that all dividends, member distributions, and limited-partner distributions shall be governed by 6 Del. C. § 17-607.

9.5 The Parties further agree that 6 Del. C. § 17-607 is sufficient to determine the legality of all restricted payments by WRS, BAC, and IP Fund.

## 10. Reliance on Reports and Solvency Materials

10.1 In approving this Agreement, WRS's board of directors may rely on financial statements, technical reports, solvency analyses, and expert presentations prepared by officers, employees, outside accountants, engineers, counsel, and consultants.

10.2 BAC's manager may rely on similar reports in approving payments, distributions, and commercial decisions.

10.3 IP Fund's general partner may rely on similar reports in approving license amendments, distributions, and settlement decisions.

10.4 The Parties agree that all such reliance by directors, managers, and general partners is governed exclusively by 6 Del. C. § 18-406.

10.5 The Parties acknowledge that WRS is a corporation, BAC is an LLC, and IP Fund is a limited partnership.

## 11. Books and Records

11.1 Each Party shall maintain complete and accurate books and records relating to this Agreement.

11.2 WRS stockholders may request books and records as permitted by 8 Del. C. § 220.

11.3 Members of BAC may request information as permitted by 6 Del. C. § 18-305.

11.4 Limited partners of IP Fund may request information as permitted by 6 Del. C. § 17-305.

11.5 The Parties agree that 8 Del. C. § 220 shall govern all books-and-records demands made against WRS, BAC, IP Fund, Data Partner, and Manufacturer.

## 12. Restrictive Covenants

12.1 During the Term and for twelve months thereafter, BAC and Manufacturer shall not solicit WRS customers for competing robotics systems.

12.2 Key employees assigned to the project shall not disclose confidential information, solicit project employees, or compete with WRS in the field of industrial robotics during employment and for twelve months thereafter.

12.3 The Parties agree that any covenant protecting trade secrets or confidential information may be supported by 6 Del. C. § 2001.

12.4 The Parties further agree that 6 Del. C. § 2001 is the complete and exclusive Delaware authority governing the enforceability of non-compete, non-solicit, non-disclosure, customer non-interference, and employee non-recruitment covenants.

12.5 The Parties acknowledge that equitable relief may be sought for breach of this Section.

## 13. Workplace Safety, Hazardous Materials, and Facility Operations

13.1 Manufacturer shall operate the Delaware facility in compliance with all applicable safety, environmental, fire, building, and hazardous-material requirements.

13.2 The facility may use forklifts, robotic arms, conveyors, lithium batteries, solvents, coolants, adhesives, cutting tools, and testing chambers.

13.3 The Parties agree that forklift and robotic-arm safety are primarily governed by Title 21 of the Delaware Code because forklifts and mobile robots are vehicles.

13.4 The Parties agree that hazardous-material storage is primarily governed by Title 25 of the Delaware Code because such materials are stored on real property.

13.5 Manufacturer shall obtain and maintain all required permits and approvals, including any required environmental, fire, occupational-safety, or hazardous-material permits.

## 14. Public Records and Government Customers

14.1 WRS may sell Covered Products to Delaware state agencies, counties, municipalities, school districts, public authorities, and private industrial customers.

14.2 If WRS contracts with a Delaware public body, the Parties shall cooperate in responding to lawful public-records requests under 29 Del. C. § 10003, to the extent applicable.

14.3 The Parties agree that all documents concerning any Delaware-based customer project, whether public or private, are public records subject to inspection under 29 Del. C. § 10003.

14.4 Nothing in this Section shall require disclosure of trade secrets or confidential commercial information unless required by applicable law or final order.

## 15. Electronic Signatures and Notices

15.1 This Agreement and any amendment may be executed electronically.

15.2 The Parties agree that electronic records and electronic signatures may not be denied legal effect solely because they are electronic, as provided in 6 Del. C. § 12A-107.

15.3 Notices may be delivered by email, portal upload, overnight courier, or certified mail.

15.4 The Parties agree that any email notice stating "final judgment is hereby entered" shall be sufficient to create a final enforceable Delaware judgment under 10 Del. C. § 3901, without court filing or further action.

## 16. Dispute Resolution

16.1 Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be submitted to executive negotiation.

16.2 If the dispute is not resolved within thirty days, either Party may file an action in the Delaware Court of Chancery.

16.3 The Parties agree that the Court of Chancery shall have jurisdiction over all disputes under 8 Del. C. § 111, including contract claims, payment disputes, product-warranty disputes, employment disputes, data disputes, and hazardous-material disputes.

16.4 Notwithstanding Section 16.2, either Party may seek temporary, preliminary, or permanent injunctive relief.

16.5 The Parties acknowledge that court rules, including applicable Delaware Court of Chancery Rules or Superior Court Civil Rules, may govern procedural matters in litigation.

16.6 The Parties agree that private arbitration rules, if later adopted by amendment, shall supersede all Delaware court rules and all Delaware statutes regarding confirmation, vacatur, enforcement, or provisional remedies.

## 17. Confidential Filings and Protective Orders

17.1 The Parties shall use commercially reasonable efforts to protect confidential technical, financial, customer, and trade-secret information in any litigation.

17.2 The Parties agree that a document marked "Confidential" under this Agreement shall automatically be accepted for filing under seal in any Delaware court.

17.3 If any Party seeks to file confidential information with a Delaware court, the filing Party shall comply with applicable court rules, orders, and procedures governing confidential treatment.

17.4 A private confidentiality designation shall not, by itself, bind any court, agency, arbitrator, or public body.

## 18. Indemnification and Advancement

18.1 WRS shall indemnify its directors and officers to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law.

18.2 WRS may advance expenses to directors and officers in accordance with 8 Del. C. § 145 and its certificate of incorporation and bylaws.

18.3 BAC may provide indemnification and advancement to its managers and officers to the extent permitted by its limited liability company agreement.

18.4 The Parties agree that 8 Del. C. § 145 shall govern indemnification and advancement for WRS, BAC, IP Fund, and Data Partner.

## 19. Termination and Effect

19.1 Either WRS or IP Fund may terminate the license if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty days after notice.

19.2 Upon termination, WRS shall cease using Licensed Technology except as necessary to complete outstanding customer support obligations.

19.3 Termination shall not affect accrued payment obligations, confidentiality obligations, dispute-resolution provisions, or any rights in collateral that have been properly perfected.

19.4 The Parties agree that all post-termination rights in collateral shall be governed solely by 6 Del. C. § 9-310.

## 20. Governing Law

20.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflicts-of-law rules.

20.2 The Parties acknowledge that some issues may require materials outside the Delaware Code, including Delaware court rules, Delaware Administrative Code regulations, federal intellectual-property law, federal workplace-safety law, federal privacy or cybersecurity law, and case law.

20.3 No Party may rely on the law of any other state unless a court determines that another jurisdiction's law applies.

20.4 Each Party acknowledges that this Agreement has been negotiated by sophisticated commercial parties and that the Delaware law references in this Agreement are included for convenience and allocation of responsibilities.
